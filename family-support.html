Common questions

How do I request learning or wellbeing support?

Contact the school using its verified contact channel and ask for the approved referral process. Do not send sensitive medical or safeguarding details through this demo form.

Where are official digital learning links?

They should be listed here once school leadership confirms the learning platforms and secure sign-in addresses.

Where do I report a safeguarding concern?

The school must publish its designated safeguarding lead and safe reporting procedure before launch. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
