TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY

Clear standards. Safer school experiences.

A policy and compliance library structure for official documents, safe reporting pathways and accessible school information.

Important: This page is a policy-library framework, not legal advice and not a statement that BrainSmart has adopted any particular policy. Do not publish it as a compliance claim until the relevant policies and contacts are approved.

Safeguarding & child protection

Publish the approved child-protection policy, designated contacts, reporting steps, visitor requirements and urgent escalation guidance.

Official document required.

Privacy & data protection

Explain what data is collected, why, retention, who can access it, third-party services, rights and contact details.

Privacy notice required before live forms.

Accessibility

Publish the accessibility commitment, how to report barriers, accommodation request process and response contact.

Accessibility contact required.

Non-discrimination & inclusion

Provide the approved equal-opportunity, anti-bullying, inclusion and complaint-resolution policies.

Approved wording required.

Records & document requests

Explain how authorised families request learner records, corrections or school documents and how identity is verified.

Records procedure required.

Website terms & third parties

Identify the site owner, external services, image permissions, cookies/analytics and how users can report broken links.

Owner and service inventory required.

Required before collecting real information

  • Publish a reviewed privacy notice and secure form handling.
  • Do not collect student health, safeguarding or identity details in unsecured demo forms.
  • Connect real forms only to an approved system with access controls and retention rules.
  • Provide verified school contact details for concerns, access needs and data requests.
  • Review every external link, image permission and third-party embed before launch.
