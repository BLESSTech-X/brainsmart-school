WHO WE ARE

A school built around possibility.

At BrainSmart School, education is more than what happens in a classroom. It is the confidence to ask, the courage to try, and the support to become.

We bring together thoughtful teaching, meaningful experiences and a caring community to help students discover their strengths and build skills for a changing world.
