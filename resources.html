Questions families often ask

Where can I confirm term dates and event times?

Use the calendar as a guide to the proposed layout, but confirm every date with the school until an approved calendar is published.

Where can I get the correct school supply list?

Contact the school for the approved list for your child's year group. The current page does not contain official lists.

Can I use the student portal with a real account?

No. The portal is a visual demonstration only and is not connected to authentication or student records. Never enter a real password.

How do I report an absence or raise a concern?

Use the contact page to identify your question. Before launch, the school should provide its official attendance and safeguarding procedures.
